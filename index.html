ABOUT MEDINTELLIX

Technology with people at its centre

Healthcare information can become scattered across reports, prescriptions, appointments, and institutions. MedIntelliX aims to make that information easier to organise and access through a unified digital experience.

Our vision is to support better-informed patients, more organised healthcare workflows, and responsible public-health insights.
